The isolated `fundamental-v2.mjs` evaluator fixes Piotroski's previous-fiscal-period lookup. The frozen original remains unchanged at `../tournament/engines/fundamental.mjs`.

The original subtracts one or two calendar years while retaining MM/DD. That requests a nonexistent February 29 in non-leap years and incorrect prior ends for 52/53-week fiscal calendars. For example, a reported December 28, 2024 fiscal end can follow December 30, 2023 and December 31, 2022. Those original dates must remain intact.

`EVALUATOR_BUGFIX_FISCAL_LINEAGE_V2` imports the independently audited `resolvePreviousFiscalPeriod` contract and resolves source-certified one- and two-step annual lineage using `security.id`, `security.issuerCik`, `security.fiscalPeriodLinks` and explicit `context.formationCutoffUTC`. It selects prior and older facts at the resolved actual period ends. Missing, malformed, conflicting, future-only or issuer-mismatched evidence blocks scoring with the resolver's reason. It never guesses a nearest period, subtracts calendar years or relabels an original fact date.

All four `FUNDAMENTAL_RULES`, original source URLs/locators, method parameters, fidelity descriptions and portfolio semantics remain JSON-identical, with identical descriptor SHA256s. Formulae, nine score signals, threshold 8, high-book-to-market conditioning before score completeness, reference quantiles, ranking, allocation, holding horizons, exclusions and execution ownership are unchanged. The new evaluator version and original-module checksum are exported separately from the rulebook.

The source-copy diff is limited to the resolver import; external implementation metadata; removal of the unused calendar-year helper; the Piotroski scoring cutoff argument and two certified lookups; the caller's cutoff argument; and clear lineage-block diagnostics. No other evaluator or shared default changes.

Validation uses synthetic independent accounting fixtures: regular December 31 lineage produces a completely identical result to the original; actual-date-preserving 53-week and leap-year fixtures score nine after the original calendar lookup blocks; malformed/future/conflicting chains remain blocked; and the other three strategies retain identical targets, scores, ranks, weights, traces and rejections. Eleven V2 tests plus eight lineage-contract and twenty original-evaluator tests pass (39 total). No historical performance or provider outcome was measured or consulted.

The legacy `selectPITFact` implementation is intentionally unchanged. The certified data/runner layer must ensure that all supplied facts are visible at the actual formation cutoff; adding a UTC lineage cutoff alone does not independently certify the availability of every accounting fact.
