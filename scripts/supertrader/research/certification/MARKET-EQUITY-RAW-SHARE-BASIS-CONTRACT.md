# Historical share observation basis: premeasurement data correction

`market-equity-certification-1.1.0` adds a fail-closed input certificate gate. An original GAAP instantaneous share fact supplies an economic observation date; it does not prove that the reported value uses raw share units at that date. Original statements may retrospectively adjust shares for a subsequent split. Multiplying an already adjusted value by the intervening split again would misstate market equity.

The receiver therefore requires `shares.observationBasis` with status `RAW_SHARE_OBSERVATION_BASIS` and basis `RAW_SHARES_ON_OBSERVATION_DATE`. It binds the exact value, unit, concept, context, observation date, raw basis date and historical issuer/listing/security identity. `splitAdjustmentTreatment` must explicitly state `NO_POST_OBSERVATION_ADJUSTMENT`, supported by an inspected original filing share disclosure or split note. Missing, unknown, later-date or retrospectively adjusted bases do not emit a market-equity fact.

Both the original share source and proof source require `ORIGINAL_SEC_FILING_BODY_VERIFIED`, a matching full received-document SHA256, original accession, URL, acceptance timestamp, context and observation date. The proof needs a concrete locator. Its publication timestamp is the original source acceptance and must be visible at formation. Source authentication and semantic disclosure inspection must come from the audited source pipeline or independent review; passing certificate metadata to this pure receiver does not independently fetch or authenticate a filing body.

The filing-identity parser does not emit this basis proof from `AS_REPORTED`, an INSTANT context, current provider bars or its number of detected facts. Existing discovered actual-share facts remain insufficient until their disclosure basis is independently established. No actual market-equity certificate was created for this correction; actual established coverage remains zero.

This is an isolated data certification correction before outcome measurement. Strategy definitions, price thresholds, sizing, portfolio rules, costs and ranking rules remain unchanged. Existing finite synthetic calculations preserve their exact values when the explicit basis certificate is supplied. Negative synthetic cases reject absent/as-reported-only/unknown bases, later split-adjusted values, future basis dates, mismatched observations and source bodies, unauthenticated source labels and publication after formation. A matched observation published later remains usable only after its real acceptance; it is never backdated.
