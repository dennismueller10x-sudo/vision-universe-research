# Isolated execution certification before unchanged-method measurement

The research version `LEGACY_KK_ENTRY_TIME_EVIDENCE_V1` confirms and gates a real lookahead in the daily fallback of legacy Momentum Breakout 3.2.0. Its inherited `kk-breakout-v3.mjs` implementation fills an intraday stop-buy at `max(session open, previously known trigger)` and initializes its stop with the completed execution-day `bars.low[t]`. That low can occur after the entry. `simulator.mjs` assigns the resulting `initialStop`; `engine/backtest.mjs` and `validation/portfolio.mjs` use entry minus that stop for position sizing. Later lows can therefore change an earlier entry's risk and number of shares. A resolved intraday oracle is a separate exception: it substitutes entry-time fill/stop observations. Daily same-bar pessimism does not remove the stop-sizing lookahead.

The synthetic regression demonstrates the issue without a historical measurement: changing only the later execution-day low changes the original stop. The isolated gate requires an independently certified instantaneous entry observation and retains the original ADR cap and stop convention. Completed execution-day high, low, close and volume cannot change this entry result. The original unfavorable end-of-day exit policy is evaluated separately after a certified closed-session clock. Date-only timestamps, unzoned times, invalid calendar rollovers and mismatched sessions are rejected. This gate is not a newly registered strategy or a complete simulator replacement; no legacy method enters the Tournament through it.

The earlier Full Tournament statement about the legacy execution-day-low issue remains correctly scoped. `MOMENTUM_BREAKOUT` was not one of the six measured participants. The separate `KULLAMAGI_DAILY_ADAPTATION` uses the already completed **SIGNAL-day** low, executes at the following open and validates that actual fill against its known stop/distance constraints. Its partial state and break-even stop change only after a successful partial execution. The confirmed legacy bug does not establish that this separate implementation used a future execution-day low. This statement does not certify all earlier terminal classifications or predict unchanged numerical outcomes after correcting source-data temporal leakage. A further legacy-only issue is documented in `engine/backtest.mjs`: intraday entry equity can use held positions' same-day closing marks. The validation portfolio's default previous marks and the Tournament's opening marks are separate paths; compatibility options must not erase this distinction.

The research accounting version `OFFICIAL_CASH_RECEIVABLE_TWO_STAGE_V2` is a mechanical isolated copy of the frozen accounting primitive with explicit corporate-action additions. A fully certified automatic cash acquisition, SPAC redemption or official zero-equity cancellation is not a discretionary sale at the last observed market price. It must not inherit a blanket terminal haircut. The legal cash entitlement becomes a nonspendable receivable at its effective timestamp; cash becomes spendable only at its independently confirmed settlement clock. Receivables enter equity and reconciliation but not tradable market exposure. An automatic distribution has no fabricated market slippage or commission; ordinary market-order costs, the cost sensitivity, sizing, caps and next-open rules remain unchanged.

The first-stage legal entitlement certificate requires independently audited historical listing/security binding, an economic action identity, exact official cash amount and units, the event's adjustment basis, official body hash and locator, and explicit source availability and actual closing/cancellation timestamps. It does not require a payment date. The separate second-stage cash settlement certificate requires an actual confirmed settlement date and payment source visible at the current phase. Unknown payment timing leaves the legal receivable nonspendable; it never forces a haircut of an exact known legal entitlement. A known settlement timestamp is preferred. An explicit conservative end-of-confirmed-settlement-date cash convention is an operational assumption, not a claim about an undocumented intraday settlement time. Approximate redemption amounts, expected settlement dates, generic bankruptcy labels, SIC codes and unverified classifier hints do not qualify. Conflicting same-listing actions are rejected atomically; a later call cannot change an already recognized action's economic binding. The validator checks these assertions and numerical/date coherence. It does not itself authenticate document contents or issuer identity.

Final, independently certified zero-equity cancellation is a separate completed economic event: realize the remaining cost and already credited dividends at the visible effective cancellation phase, with zero cash and fees. It creates neither a fictitious payment date nor a permanent zero-valued pending-settlement claim. Generic bankruptcy, a low price or contingent/noncash recovery is not this certificate.

The integration must process available official actions before pending next-open orders and again after the close, including sessions without pending orders. An action effective before the open blocks a stale pending re-entry. A known but not-yet-effective cash event suppresses a false terminal haircut while retaining the last observed trading mark until the effective phase; it does not create cash early. Ordinary missing or zero opens remain rejected, without carry-forward fills or partial-state mutation. Ordinary exits precede requested increases, and original proportional cash-shortfall scaling remains intact. Fixed-horizon signal outcomes must use the same certified payout basis as portfolio settlement, with no invented cash yield.

The existing R15 SEC metadata cases are reused as five classifier regressions only. The frozen loader still consumes R13 hints, while R15 also recognizes SPAC-trust and deregistration/no-distress patterns. Neither establishes realized cash proceeds or their time. Four official SEC submission records and four primary filing bodies were retrieved in this pass and retained privately with their actual body hashes. They provide useful discovery evidence but do not establish any complete official cash certificate: approximate/expected redemption and incomplete settlement information remain open. No actual payout ledger has been admitted.

Data quarantine is an additional certification limit. The original runner can supply a synthetic last mark to portfolio terminal scenarios, while its corresponding fixed-horizon signal observation is censored. This is an experimental scenario convention, not evidence of an economic delisting or realized proceeds. The isolated helper labels quarantine outcomes uncertified. It preserves original unknown-proceeds scenarios for no-certificate parity; a certification admission gate must not describe those scenarios as reconstructed actual cash settlements.

Sixteen synthetic regression tests pass. They cover the original legacy counterexample, future-bar invariance, late-phase timestamp guards, no-certificate accounting parity, unavailable-open handling, receivable equity/reconciliation, dated settlement and replay, conflicting event bindings, arithmetic overflow, pending event timing, pre-open re-entry rejection, R15 hint scope and certified terminal endpoints. Original frozen file integrity passes. No live files, original frozen methods, costs or thresholds were changed, and no performance measurement was run. Operational primitive verification does not certify the historical market universe, listing identities, actual terminal ledger or intraday data.

The additional `TERMINAL_CLASS_PIT_FILTER_V1` data overlay strips R13 classes supported by post-terminal or undated evidence, and same-day date-only evidence without an exact validated publication clock. It changes no method weight, cost or unknown-proceeds scenario. A metadata-only diagnostic found 2,263 observed listing rows with R13 hints, versus 2,265 entries in the raw source dictionary: these are different count units. At the hints' metadata list-end dates, 297 nonunknown class hints are conservatively stripped. These counts are not a replay against actual last-bar terminal timestamps and do not measure affected Tournament outcomes. The actual terminal cutoff must be applied before measurement. Later confirmed payments cannot alter earlier decisions.
