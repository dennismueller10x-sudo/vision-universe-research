# Private native Close chart admission

`europe-discover-chart-quality.mjs` evaluates an explicitly requested display range independently of OHLC, volume, adjusted fields, analytical engines and strategy admission. Discover uses a separately authenticated identity and the safe default **1Y** chart. Other ranges have their own proofs; an older unexplained break cannot silently make MAX available.

The minimum is 20 genuine observations spanning at least 30 calendar days. A reasonably complete year means at least 200 observations and 330 days; smaller useful histories remain `CHART_LIMITED`. Missing or excluded sessions split the points into explicit segments with `breakBefore` markers. Prices, dates and gaps are never estimated or repaired. A one-step ratio of at least 2 or at most 0.5 is a serious discontinuity and blocks that range unless an exactly scoped source-backed split explains it. An explained split annotates and separates raw prices; it never adjusts them. Unknown action coverage alone does not block native Close charts.

The last completed session comes from an exact-MIC authenticated exchange schedule evaluated at an explicit timestamp. Session lag zero is `CURRENT_LAST_SESSION`; one to three sessions is `DELAYED`, with chart status limited at two or three. More than three is `STALE` and blocked. Missing schedule or unmeasurable age is `UNKNOWN` and blocked.

Every known prior RAW quarantine date, including Review histories and latest observations, is excluded before extracting Close. A fresh or changed response on that date cannot release it. Positive Close does not override a prior OHLC/volume quarantine. New Close admission requires positive finite raw Close, a real completed trading date, exact reported symbol and MIC, exact response and request-range bindings, and no duplicate or contradictory observations. Equal observations from different authenticated operations retain all source references; duplicates inside one operation remain critical.

Quotation currency is independently bound to the exact listing. Missing provider currency may use an exact official listing reference; foreign listings cannot inherit Xetra currency. A nonnull provider ISO currency field can supply native quotation currency with the official OpenAPI schema and exact raw-row evidence, labelled `PROVIDER_EXPLICIT_QUOTE_CURRENCY`. This grants no universal provider major-unit or analytical certification. ISO currency case comparison preserves the original provider value. GBP/GBX requires a separate documented unit contract; no pence assumption or currency conversion is made.

The pure helper verifies input shapes and evidence bindings. An upstream producer must authenticate complete source bytes, source manifests, identity facts and immutable exclusion inventories. SHA-looking input fields alone do not authenticate a provider. Private analysis preserves the full original raw archives and pins their hashes; compact projections retain the exact raw-row and source-file references.

Outputs remain `RAW_UNADJUSTED`, with OHLC, volume, adjustment, Technical, Quant, Backtest and publication certification all false. Rights and public display admission are independent gates. No formulas, engines, US population or schedules are modified.
