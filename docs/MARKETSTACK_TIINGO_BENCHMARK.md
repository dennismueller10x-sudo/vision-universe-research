Measured on 2026-10-01 against the accepted retained US baseline. The complete bounded latest-EOD run checked all 6,738 exact API-directory matches and accepted 6,278 latest observations. Tiingo remains operational and authoritative for its existing production roles; replacement is not justified by this evidence.

## Corporate actions and historical basis

Actual EOD windows and separate split/dividend endpoints agree on NVDA's 10:1 split on 2024-06-10 and dividend 0.01 on 2024-06-11; AAPL's 4:1 split on 2020-08-31; and SPY's dividend 1.89 on 2026-09-18. These windows do not establish complete corporate-action coverage.

The historical price fields are not safely interchangeable with the existing Tiingo raw-price contract:

- AAPL pre-split OHLC is already divided by four, while the response still carries `split_factor=4`. On 2020-08-24, Marketstack's named raw close is 125.8575 and canonical Tiingo raw close is 503.43. Applying the existing split reconstruction would adjust twice. Pre-split volume is approximately four times Tiingo raw volume.
- NVDA pre-split OHLC agrees with Tiingo raw OHLC, while named raw volume is approximately ten times Tiingo raw volume. A single universal adjustment assumption is therefore unsafe.
- The strict normalization gate rejects three AAPL historical candles for inconsistent adjusted OHLC: 2020-08-25, 2020-08-26 and 2020-09-01. The last has observed adjusted close 132.1582 exceeding adjusted high 131.2853990978. NVDA 2024-06-03 has a smaller adjusted close/high rounding conflict and is also reported explicitly.
- Across the two golden historical windows, 16 accepted dates contain 19 field discrepancies: 12 AAPL OHLC differences and seven pre-split volume differences. These are consistent with mixed split bases rather than an adjudicated price error.
- Accepted AAPL historical adjusted-close observations differ by approximately 1.596% from current canonical total-return values. NVDA observations differ by at most approximately 0.276%. Retrieval timing and provider methodology remain possible explanations; neither source is automatically declared correct.

Marketstack adjusted fields remain `UNVERIFIED` observations. Canonical `adjustedClose` stays null. The analyzer records split-basis signatures and refuses basis-sensitive routing. No canonical history or corporate action is overwritten.

## Intraday and live evidence

The unqualified historical NVDA intraday request returned 115 five-minute bars on IEXG for 2026-09-29, spanning 12:00 through 21:30 UTC. Thirty-one bars omit volume. This proves historical `INTRADAY` availability for that request, including times outside the regular US session; it does not prove complete session coverage, consolidated volume, current latency or realtime equivalence. The same requested window returned no 2026-09-30 bars.

Qualified `intraday/latest` checks for NVDA/XNAS and SPY/ARCX returned empty responses. Snapshot calls returned HTTP 404. These observations may reflect endpoint/venue semantics and must not be generalized into unsupported realtime claims. Tiingo's existing IEX/runtime infrastructure remains intact. No Marketstack LIVE label is authorized by this probe.

The supplemental run correctly qualified US latest intraday by IEXG and returned one NVDA and one SPY observation timestamped 2026-09-30 at 20:00 UTC; SAP.DE/XETR remained empty. Returned `close` values were 227.21 and 764.2, equal to the preceding day's EOD values, while `marketstack_last` values were 228.67 and 762.36. Therefore `close` cannot be treated as the current spot price without resolving field semantics. These overnight observations do not measure open-session refresh, latency or realtime equivalence. Unfiltered snapshot requests returned multiple venue observations and likewise require identity selection.

Actions run [36856401441](https://github.com/dennismueller10x-sudo/vision-universe-research/actions/runs/36856401441) also queried the preserved Tiingo quote adapter for NVDA, AAPL and SPY. All three returned the previous US closing-session timestamp, 2026-09-30 at 20:00 UTC, during the overnight probe. Tiingo reference prices were 228.38, 333.02 and 762.63; NVDA's Marketstack latest intraday reference was 228.67. The approximately 0.127% NVDA difference does not adjudicate either source. Transport latencies were measured, but an overnight fetch duration does not measure market-data latency. Neither provider's realtime refresh or active-session equivalence was established by these calls.

## Economics, validation and decision

The complete first multi-region probe attempted 104 HTTP requests, including five retries, with 199 conservatively estimated credits. The supplemental probe attempted 184 requests, including three retries, with 222 conservatively estimated credits. These totals cover entire multi-region probes, not just US comparisons. The estimates are not the account balance or measured billing debits. Public directory analysis used no authenticated credits.

The final whole matched-US latest benchmark alone used 69 attempts and 6,738 estimated symbol credits. Under the conservative per-symbol accounting model, repeating it on 20–22 trading days would consume approximately 134,760–148,236 credits per month, exceeding the current 100,000-credit Professional allocation before other regions or ETF requests. Actual account billing was not independently measured. Consequently the full run is a controlled benchmark, not an uncapped daily schedule; recurring coverage, cadence and incremental request economics require a separate measured budget decision.

US-specific implementation tests cover listing identity, currency quarantine, symbol/MIC matching, excluded baseline preservation, missing/duplicate candles, adjustment trust, split-basis signatures, resumed directory completeness, cumulative latest-run budgets and public artifact redaction. Nine pre-existing relevant suites passed 173 baseline tests; the five new US benchmark/summary/directory/latest suites pass 23 tests. Existing real golden histories passed the established quality gate with zero findings across 14,760 bars. The primary run separately executes the wider regression suite.

Retain Tiingo. Current evidence does not support a complete or primary-provider US migration: incomplete catalog identity matches, explicit currency/venue conflicts, mixed historical split bases, inconsistent adjusted OHLC and unproven current/live equivalence remain unresolved. Marketstack may supply independently validated additive listings through the canonical seam while these issues are investigated. Any future migration requires complete authenticated matching, broader quality samples, exchange-qualified anomaly resolution, a proven price-basis contract and current-session measurements.
