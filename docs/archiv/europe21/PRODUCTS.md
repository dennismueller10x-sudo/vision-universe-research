# Europe 2.1 product contracts

The product integration is opt-in and remains private unless concrete display and commercial rights cover each data path. US securities, identifiers, storage, Tiingo routing, ranking populations and strategies retain their existing behavior. No provider requests, licensed data publication or schedules are introduced here.

`scripts/marketstack/europe-product-readiness.mjs` provides two projections:

- `auditEuropeCanonicalGraph` verifies company, ISIN share class and listing membership, explicit primary/secondary facts, activity, MIC/provider symbol/currency, official identity dimensions, Core graph coverage and protected identifiers. A previous universe can prove that accepted IDs were retained. Unknown ordinary/preferred class detail remains `UNKNOWN`; separate securities belonging to the same company remain separate results.
- `projectEuropeProductPublication` reports each product independently. Actual identity, search, persisted watchlist, chart, technical and screener consumer results are required. A readiness label alone does not prove that a consumer worked. Raw limited charts and partial existing-engine technical metrics may be `READY_PRIVATE`; their qualifications remain visible in `dataStatus`. Missing RS stays blocked separately and missing metrics remain null. Input-ready Quant, SuperTrader and Backtest retain `BLOCKED_METHOD` for publication until the appropriate Europe product population exists.

`privateStatus`, `publicStatus` and `publicationStatus` are separate. Open rights produce `READY_PRIVATE` plus `publicStatus: BLOCKED_RIGHTS` for otherwise usable products. A commercial-plan statement alone cannot produce `READY_PUBLIC`: confirmation must name an executed contract or provider written confirmation, be verified, match the evidence reference and authorize every required data path. Raw and canonical price paths are distinct. Public limited charts additionally require evidence that their limitations are visible. Partial raw technical research remains private; confirmed rights or a limitation label cannot relax the existing strict public technical gate. The projection reports eligibility; it does not authorize, load or publish data.

The private replay verifies compiler file SHA-256 and bytes, uses the real Core adapter and exercises every admitted security. It tests source-verified aliases and mixed watchlists in batches of at most the existing 500-item cap. Country/exchange queries validate canonical result membership and duplicates while disclosing the existing 100-result search cap. Broad metadata queries do not claim exhaustive membership. Every admitted history is exercised through the actual price-series contract; unavailable histories are never synthesized. Screener and technical publication are based on actual consumer responses, independently of simple chart usability.

The known canonical identity graph and the currently admitted consumer universe are separate producer outputs. Retaining a previous identity never promotes a current review candidate through the product bridge. The consumer replay must receive the final admitted-only universe and corresponding source-bound projection. Optional `referenceUniverse` is materialized separately through an identity-only Core catalog and must retain every previous canonical ID. The current admitted graph retains shared ISIN IDs and must match the reference company/security identity. Reference-only securities receive no price, search, watchlist or product consumer invocation. Without an explicit reference graph, the previous-universe audit continues to require full membership. This split prevents a current admission failure from erasing identity or accidentally promoting an unsafe listing.
