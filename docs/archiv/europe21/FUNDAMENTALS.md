# Europe 2.1 official fundamentals mapping

`scripts/marketstack/europe-fundamentals-mapping.mjs` produces private readiness
for every accepted canonical security. It does not change SEC, Tiingo, Quant
methods, existing scores or any ranking population. Provider fundamentals and
ADR financial inputs are not used.

The resolver recomputes source SHA256 from original bytes. Exact GLEIF
ISIN-filter responses bind an active general issuer, LEI, legal jurisdiction and
registry identity. Prior observations retain their actual retrieval date and
are explicitly dated; they are not labelled as current observations.

Official French OAM records bind ISIN, declared issuer LEI, publication time and
the exact official package URL. Pinned PR #324 is still a draft; it is not
merged by this work. Its isolated Arelle parser is executed privately with the
unchanged current-main SEC concept registry. The private runner supplies only
the missing country/JSON IO utilities; it does not change normalization.

The mapper independently checks the actual package's bounded report XHTML:
non-dimensional LEI contexts, instant/duration dates, fiscal closing year,
registry metric/concept correspondence, source units, numeric observations,
sign and scale. Duplicate context/unit declarations, conflicts and unsupported
XML/transform shapes withhold facts. Packages are capped at 32 MiB compressed
and 128 MiB expanded, without filesystem extraction or external XML entities.

Company-level financial facts remain `CONSOLIDATED_ISSUER_RESEARCH`. EPS and
share counts require separate local share-class evidence and are withheld.
Report currency is retained; differing quote/report currencies do not trigger
conversion. Fiscal periods are explicit; annual facts do not become invented
quarterly or TTM inputs. No `FUNDAMENTALS_FULL`, financial engine readiness,
ranking admission or public publication is issued by this mapper.

The narrow historical identity bridge accepts only a current GLEIF `DUPLICATE`
old LEI with an explicit single successor to the current issuer, identical
registry authority/identifier and legal jurisdiction. Both records must be
observed on the requested day. Current issuer LEI, declared historical LEI and
actual reporting-context LEI remain separate. This does not verify historical
share classes or permit ADR transfer.
